Quantitative Research

Volatility Risk Premium (VRP) Study & Pipeline

Medium Article ↗ Streamlit App ↗ GitHub ↗

A self-contained empirical research pipeline in Python analyzing S&P 500 (SPX) and VIX price history from 2010 to present to determine whether the options market systematically overprices forward fear.

$$\text{VRP}_t = \text{VIX}_{\text{dec}, t} - \text{RealizedVol}_{30\text{d forward}, t}$$
  • Forward Realized Volatility Alignment: Calculated 30-day forward annualized realized volatility ($RV = \text{rolling\_std}(r_t, 30) \times \sqrt{252}$ shifted backward by 30 trading days), proving that VRP is positive on >85% of trading days.
  • Regime Segmentation: Segmented market conditions into Bull ($VIX < 20\% \land SPX > 200d\text{ MA}$), Crisis ($VIX \ge 30\%$), and Bear. Proved that VRP sharply inverts during acute systemic panics (e.g. March 2020 COVID crash, Feb 2018 Volmageddon).
  • Variance Swap Simulation: Simulated monthly variance swap payoff models ($PnL = w \cdot [VIX^2 - RV^2]$) with volatility-inverse sizing, leverage caps, and circuit breakers; deployed as an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
