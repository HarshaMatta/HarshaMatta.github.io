Volatility Risk Premium (VRP) Study & Pipeline

Quantitative options pricing pipeline & interactive strategy simulation

  • Built an end-to-end 5-phase data ingestion and research pipeline in Python analyzing daily SPX and VIX price history from 2010 to present.
  • Aligned 30-day forward annualized realized volatility (RV = std(r_t, 30) × √252) against implied VIX, verifying that VRP is positive on >85% of trading days.
  • Partitioned market regimes (Bull, Crisis, Bear) and proved that VRP inverts during acute systemic crashes (e.g. March 2020 COVID shock, Feb 2018 Volmageddon).
  • Simulated monthly variance swap payoff models (PnL = w · [VIX² - RV²]) with inverse-volatility sizing, leverage ceilings, and VIX circuit breakers; deployed as an interactive Streamlit app.
